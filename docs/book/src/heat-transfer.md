# Heat Transfer

- **Convection**: Dittus-Boelter, Sieder-Tate, laminar limits (3.66 /
  4.36), flat-plate correlations, series wall resistances.
- **Exchangers**: LMTD (with the Bowman 1-shell-pass F factor), ε-NTU
  for counter/co-current and 1-shell-pass, rating and sizing.
- **Pinch analysis**: problem-table cascade, composite and grand
  composite curves, minimum-utility targets, pinch detection (threshold
  problems report no pinch), interval-based network synthesis.
- **Fired equipment**: combustion stoichiometry with excess air,
  stack-loss efficiency.
