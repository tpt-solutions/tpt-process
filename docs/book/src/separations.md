# Separations

- **Distillation**: Fenske-Underwood-Gilliland shortcut, McCabe-Thiele
  stepping at constant α (cross-validated against FUG), and a
  Lewis-Sorel stage-to-stage MESH simulation.
- **Absorption/stripping**: Kremser equation with the A = 1 linear limit.
- **Extraction**: crosscurrent batteries with a constant distribution
  coefficient.
- **Membranes**: solution-diffusion gas flux, GPU permeance, RO water
  flux with osmotic pressure, salt rejection.
- **Crystallization**: cooling yields from solubility curves, MSMPR
  dominant size and nucleation.
